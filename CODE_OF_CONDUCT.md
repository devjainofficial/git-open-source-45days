# Code of Conduct (Short)

We are a learning community. Be kind, be curious, and assume good intent.

## Standards
- Use welcoming, inclusive language
- Respect different experience levels and backgrounds
- Offer constructive feedback; avoid personal attacks
- No harassment or hate speech

## Reporting
If you witness unacceptable behavior, open a confidential Issue titled "Code of Conduct" or contact the maintainer privately.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may take appropriate action, including warnings or removal of content.
